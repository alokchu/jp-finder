Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how NSW JP Finder (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal information when you use jpfinder.com.au (“the Site”).

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: We collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including through our contact forms (e.g. name, email address), or if you submit feedback or requests.

2. Use of Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Site and our services to you.

  • To monitor, analyse, and improve user experience, website performance, and content.
  • For security purposes, including protecting against fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with Australian law and regulatory obligations.

Our site uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. We use Google Analytics to collect anonymous statistics on how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics may collect information such as your IP address (in anonymised form), browser type, and pages visited. For details, please review How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

By using our site, you consent to the processing of data about you by Google in the manner and for the purposes set out above.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, some features of our Site may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

We may display Google AdSense advertising. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings.

Our site may include links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.

5. Data Security

We take commercially reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. While no method of transmission over the Internet is perfectly secure, we strive to follow industry best practices to safeguard your data.

6. Disclosure of Personal Information

  • Your personal data is not sold, traded, or otherwise transferred to outside parties except to trusted third parties who assist us in operating the Site, conducting our business, or servicing you (e.g., web hosting, analytics), as long as those parties agree to keep this information confidential and comply with privacy laws.

7. Access, Correction, and Deletion

You may request access to your personal information that we hold and ask us to correct or delete it, subject to Australian privacy law. Please contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

This site is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
