About NSW JP Finder

NSW JP Finder is an independent online community service designed to help individuals and organisations easily locate free Justice of the Peace (JP) services across New South Wales.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make accessing JP services as simple and convenient as possible. Whether you need to have documents witnessed, affidavits signed, or certified copies made, our directory connects you with local councils, libraries, and community venues where accredited JPs are available.

Why Use NSW JP Finder?

  • Clear, updated information on JP sessions by location, council, and type of service
  • User-focused design for both mobile and desktop visitors
  • Independence and privacy – we do not sell, trade, or misuse your data
  • Free resource for the NSW community

Who We Serve

Members of the public, legal professionals, students, and anyone needing official documents witnessed or certified can benefit from our platform. We aim to support people from all backgrounds, whether you live in Sydney, regional NSW, or are just passing through.

How We Operate

We aggregate publicly available information and user feedback to keep our listings up to date. We encourage users and council staff to contact us with suggestions, corrections, or updates to improve our service.

Disclaimer

NSW JP Finder is an independent directory and is not affiliated with the NSW Government or any official body. For the most accurate and official information, always check with the relevant council or the NSW Government Justice of the Peace website.

Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or wish to contribute, email us at jpfinder.aus@gmail.com or use our contact form.

Together, we make it easier for everyone in NSW to access Justice of the Peace services.
